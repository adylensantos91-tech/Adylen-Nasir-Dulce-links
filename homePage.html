What is the World Cup?

the world cup is something fifa does every four years taking place in 1930 in Uruguay. Since then, the competition has been held every four years, except for interruptions caused by World War II.

And 48 countries compete against each other to win like in 2014 Germany won the cup,2018 fracnce won the cup,in 2022 argentina won the cup,and now in 2026 spain won the world cup.

The FIFA World Cup is an international soccer tournament where the best countries compete to become world champions. It is held every four years and is one of the most watched sporting events in the world. The tournament brings together millions of fans to support their favorite teams. Winning the World Cup is considered the greatest achievement in international soccer. The World Cup helps bring people from different countries together through their love of soccer. Many famous players dream of winning the World Cup because it is the highest honor in the sport.

What steps teams have to do to get in the world cup:

Teams must first play qualifying matches against other countries in their continent. They earn points by winning or tying games. The teams with the best records qualify for the FIFA World Cup. At the World Cup, teams compete in the group stage. The best teams then advance to the knockout rounds and try to win the championship.
